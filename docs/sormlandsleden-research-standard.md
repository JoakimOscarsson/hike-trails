# Sörmlandsleden Research Standard

Every Sörmlandsleden section should be enriched from more than the official trail page before it is treated as planning-grade.

Required source categories:

- Official Sörmlandsleden section page and current notices.
- Naturkartan stage and facility pages where available.
- Municipality, county board, nature reserve, and national park rule pages.
- Published guidebooks or hiking books when available.
- Hiker blogs and trip reports.
- Forum threads and recent comments, especially for water, shelter condition, reroutes, and safety.
- Map/service sources for coordinates, transit, parking, toilets, food, and opening hours.
- Recent reviews from hiking/map services when they add condition detail.

Each researched facility should answer:

- Exact or best-known coordinates.
- Facility type shown on the map.
- Whether it is seasonal, unreliable, private, paid, or opening-hour dependent.
- Camping and fire rules for that exact area.
- Water quality/reliability, especially when the source is natural or untested.
- Source URL and confidence level.

Current status:

- Roslagsleden and Sörmlandsleden stages 1-10 have deeper facility enrichment.
- Sörmlandsleden stages beyond 10 are imported from the official 94-stage catalog and need this full enrichment pass before their facilities should be treated as complete.
