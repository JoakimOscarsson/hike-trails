## Import Notes

- Model `difficulty` separately from `exposure`; several routes are easy in calm weather and advanced in wind or leftover swell.
- Add `seasonalRestrictions` for bird protection and reserve camping/fire rules.
- Gålö and Öja-Landsort have especially explicit reserve constraints.
